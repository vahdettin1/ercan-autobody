The Art of Restoration

Founded by Master Painter Ahmet Ercan

Located in the heart of London, Ercan's Premium Auto Body is not just a repair shop; it's a studio where automotive art is brought back to life. With over 20 years of hands-on experience, Ahmet Ercan has built a reputation for excellence.

From high-end supercars to beloved daily drivers, we treat every vehicle with the exact same level of prestige and respect. We don't just fix cars—we erase history.

Our Premium Services

State-of-the-art equipment meets old-world craftsmanship.

Collision Repair

Complete structural and cosmetic repairs following major impacts. We bring the chassis and body panels back to exact factory specifications.

Custom Paint & Respray

Computerized color matching and dust-free baking booths ensure a mirror-like, flawless finish that lasts a lifetime.

Paintless Dent Removal

Carefully massaging dents out of panels without compromising the original paintwork. Fast, effective, and invisible.

Ceramic Coating

Ultimate protection for your new paint. Hydrophobic layers that protect against scratches, UV rays, and London weather.

Alloy Wheel Refurbishment

Curbed or scratched your premium alloys? We restore them to showroom condition with custom powder coating options.

Classic Restoration

Breathing new life into vintage legends. Rust removal, panel fabrication, and period-correct paint applications.
